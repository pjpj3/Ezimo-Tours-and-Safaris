About Ezimo

Ezimo Tours & Safaris is a Namibian-owned tour operator offering guided safaris, small-group travel and ground operations across Namibia and Southern Africa. “Ezimo” means family, and that is how we approach every trip.

Our itineraries are well paced and our service is personal, led by local guides who know the land and the realities of travel here. We also work with local artisans and communities, so your trip supports the people who share it with you.
